Durable message processing, opt-in through `BotConfig.durability`. Without the key nothing changes. Inbound messages and interactions are journaled in an inbox before dispatch and acknowledged once their conversation steps settle; after a crash they are replayed on the next `start()`. Every send goes through an outbox under an idempotency key (`SendOptions.idempotencyKey`, or derived from the inbound message being handled), so a replay never re-sends a reply that already went out. Retries use backoff and honour `RateLimitedError`; there is a dead-letter queue, `SendDeferredError` once `outbox.inlineMaxMs` runs out, leases with fencing tokens (`StaleLeaseError`) and an `onAmbiguous` policy. New API: `bot.durability`, `bot.enqueue()`, and the `'durability:dead'` / `'durability:ambiguous'` events. Two backends ship in core, `MemoryDurability` (default) and `FileDurability`; store packages implement the `DurableKv` seam through `KvDurability`. `FlowCheckpoint` gains the optional `inboundId` and `cause` fields. `@bot-wa/core/testing` adds `crashable()` and `CrashableProvider`. Several bots can share one backend: each works in the view of its namespace (`DurabilityBackend.scope()`, `bot.durability.scope`), so claims, replays and explicit idempotency keys are per bot. On a shared backend the default `instanceId` is unique per boot (own leases are reclaimed early only with an explicit id or a single-process backend). A message that keeps killing the process is dead-lettered after `inbox.maxAttempts` instead of crash-looping, sends in flight extend their outbox lease (`OutboxJournal.extend`), live events of one chat keep their arrival order while the inbox append runs, and a replay whose n-th send has different content than the recorded one is sent under a new key (`outbox.onDiverged: 'send' | 'skip'`, metric `outboxDiverged`). With `BotConfig.coordination`, the outbox sweeper only calls the transport on a replica that holds the provider session (a standby's attempt is refused with `ProviderNotLeaderError` and retried by the leader), and the shared inbox journal deduplicates redeliveries across replicas. A live send whose outbox record the sweeper claims meanwhile follows that delivery instead of throwing `SendDeferredError`.
