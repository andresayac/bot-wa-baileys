Opt-in multi-replica coordination (`BotConfig.coordination`). A `Coordinator` (exported contract, with `MemoryCoordinator`/`MemoryCoordinationHub` as the in-process reference) gives each chat a cross-replica FIFO: the Dispatcher reserves a ticket per message at enqueue time and processes it only while holding the chat's turn, held until the conversation it fed settles. A message that cannot get its turn is dropped and reported (`CoordinationUnavailableError`); sends after a lost turn reject with `LeaseLostError`. A `.persistent()` flow continues on another replica from its checkpoint, the waking message answering the pending question. New optional `ProviderCapabilities.exclusiveSession` and `coordination.providerSession`: exclusive transports start only on the elected replica (`ProviderNotLeaderError` on standbys). New events `'coordination:leader'`, `'coordination:follower'`, `'coordination:lease-lost'`; `MockProvider` gains `exclusiveSession`, `starts` and `stops`. Conversation timers (wait timeouts, `c.idle()`) run under the chat's turn; a handover answers the pending question only when the checkpoint says the flow was parked on its step's first wait (new optional `FlowCheckpoint.parked`); a step longer than `settleMs` is suspended before the turn is released; `coordination.providerDownMs` makes a leader with a dead transport step down. Without `coordination` nothing changes.
