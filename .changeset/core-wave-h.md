Inbound locations: `InboundMessage.location` (`{ latitude, longitude, name?, address?, live? }`, type `InboundLocation`) surfaced as `ctx.sharedLocation` (`ctx.location()` keeps sending one), the `toInboundLocation()` helper for providers, `MockProvider.incomingLocation()` / the `location` option of `incoming()` and `TestChat.sendLocation()`. `PluginSpec.requires` is enforced: `bot.plug()` throws a descriptive error before `install()` when a required plugin is not plugged. Flows a plugin registers through `ctx.bot.registerFlow()` (or creates with `replaceFlow()`) are unregistered on unplug. A message dropped because the bot stopped before its turn is no longer recorded by inbound dedupe, so its redelivery after a restart is processed (`Dispatcher.dispatch()` resolves `false` for it). `bot.resetWelcome()` can no longer be undone by a first-contact check in flight. Cron now follows standard semantics: day-of-month and weekday are OR-ed when both are restricted (`0 9 13 * 5` = every 13th and every Friday), and a `*` step counts from the field minimum (`*/3` months = Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct) — a behaviour change for expressions that relied on the old AND rule or zero-based steps.
