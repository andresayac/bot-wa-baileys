Multi-replica conversation hand-over. With `coordination` and `durability`, an inbound record that fed a conversation step is acknowledged only after the checkpoint write holding its effect landed; a step cut short after its first wait consumed an answer records that answer (`FlowCheckpoint.answer`) so the replica that takes the chat continues with it instead of asking again (a failed write releases the record so it is replayed); checkpoint writes are fenced by the chat turn (a local check, not a conditional write), and a suspended step that keeps running never writes or clears the checkpoint afterwards, even once the same replica took the chat again; a suspended step that consumed an answer no checkpoint holds releases its record instead of acknowledging it. New opt-in `coordination.handover`: `flows: 'forward'` hands non-persistent flows over through TTL-bound ephemeral checkpoints (never expiring before the explicit timeout of the wait they park on; cleared by a replica without `forward` that resumes one — upgrade every replica before enabling it), and `fallback: 'notify' | 'restart' | 'drop' | fn` handles conversations that cannot be forwarded (async `bot.conversation`). New `'coordination:handover'` event, `core.conversation.interrupted` built-in message and types `CheckpointAnswer`, `CheckpointHandover`, `ConversationHandoverConfig`, `ConversationHandoverEvent`, `HandoverFallback`, `HandoverContext`, `InterruptedConversation`. With coordination, persistent flows are also checkpointed on entry. Without `coordination` nothing changes.
