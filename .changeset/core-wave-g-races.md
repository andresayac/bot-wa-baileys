Fix races and lifecycle edge cases: a matcher registered while a message is being routed no longer makes another matcher run twice; entering a conversation discards that key's dormant checkpoint (the stale flow used to resume on the next message); `start()` reloads dormant checkpoints from the store instead of merging them with the pre-`stop()` ones; `bot.deleteSubject()` cancels the subject's live and dormant conversations; a `stop()`/`start()` while a scheduled task runs no longer forks its timer chain or re-runs a `delay()` task; delivery receipts never move a message's status backwards and are written in order per message; `bot.broadcast()` before `start()` sends nothing. `bot.plug()`'s unplug now also removes conversations, flows and nested plugins registered through `ctx.bot` and calls `uninstall()` once; a throwing `install()` is rolled back and a rejected async one goes to the `'error'` event. Cron: stepped ranges (`1-5/2`) honour their upper bound, `7` means Sunday and a malformed expression throws a `TypeError` naming the field. `c.choose()` with no options throws a clear error. New `ConversationRuntime.abortWhere()`.
